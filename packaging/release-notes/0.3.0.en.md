• Voice calls: talk to your friends on Eleza for Android, iPhone and desktop, from the top of any one-to-one chat or from a profile.
• Incoming calls ring even when the Eleza window is closed or minimized; press Enter to answer, Esc to decline.
• New "Calls" settings: choose the microphone and speaker, test the microphone, noise and echo removal, and who can call you.
• Finished, unanswered and missed calls are kept in the chat; one click calls back.
